Multi-arch image on Docker Hub: alexlinos/threat-feed-me:latest. On first start it fetches the 18 feeds (15 IP feeds plus URLhaus, OpenPhish, and HaGeZi domain feeds), dedupes and scores them, and begins serving block lists, then auto-refreshes every 60 minutes. Opt-in integrations with free API keys: HoneyDB (honeypot sensor network, including sensors you operate voting as a source) and AlienVault OTX. Prefer compose? See the README.

#Confidence tiers

Every deduped IP is scored and published as a live feed URL. Paste the tier that fits your risk appetite into your firewall.

High strictest

More than two independent sources agree (overlap-discounted), threat-intel validated. The cleanest list.

/feeds/high.txt

Medium recommended

Corroborated by more than one independent source. The balanced default most firewalls should poll.

/feeds/medium.txt

Everything widest net

Every deduped indicator with any evidence, including your custom lists.

/feeds/all.txt
  • Is it a real agreement? Two feeds that are mostly the same list are one witness, not two, so overlapping lists get discounted. spamhaus + et + bbcan177 agreeing on a DROP range is worth ~1.1 votes, not 3.
  • Is the witness reliable? Every feed starts at full weight. Flag a false positive and that feed's weight drops, so its IPs score lower until you stop seeing mistakes.
  • Is the sighting fresh? A scan from three days ago matters less than one from an hour ago. Age halves an indicator's score every 72 hours.

Tiers come from the shape of the crowd. Every IP gets one number: how many genuinely independent, reputable, fresh witnesses it has. The tier lines are drawn at natural gaps in that distribution, and held stable so live firewall IPs don't reshuffle as feeds cycle in and out. They only move when the vote distribution actually shifts, or a feed is added, removed, or resized.

Each URL returns plain text, one entry per line, generated live with the whitelist applied. The same tiers exist for malicious domains at /feeds/domains/high.txt, medium, and all — for the DNS-filter side of the firewall (or Pi-hole / AdGuard Home). IP URLs never contain a domain and vice versa. .csv and .json variants are available for SIEM use.
